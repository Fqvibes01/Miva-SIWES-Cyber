Part 1: Understanding Digital Forensics

Digital forensics is the process of identifying, preserving, extracting, and analyzing digital evidence from devices to present in a court of law. It is crucial because it uncovers deleted files, reconstructs timelines, and provides objective proof of a suspect's actions.

Steps of Digital Investigation:
- Identification: Locating the evidence.
- Preservation: Securing the data (creating a forensic image) so it isn't altered.
- Analysis: Examining the data to answer who, what, when, and how.
- Presentation: Documenting findings clearly for non-technical audiences (like a jury).
