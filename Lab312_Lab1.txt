## Lab 1: Basic Networking Concepts

Part 1: OSI and TCP/IP Models
The OSI model has 7 layers (Physical, Data Link, Network, Transport, Session, Presentation, Application). The TCP/IP model condenses this into 4 layers (Network Access, Internet, Transport, Application). 

Part 2: IP Addressing
IPv4 uses 32-bit addresses, while IPv6 uses 128-bit addresses. Public IPs are routable on the internet, while private IPs (like 192.168.x.x) are used inside local networks and require NAT to access the internet.

Part 3: Network Devices
Routers operate at Layer 3 (Network) and forward packets between different networks. Switches operate at Layer 2 (Data Link) and forward frames based on MAC addresses within a local network.
